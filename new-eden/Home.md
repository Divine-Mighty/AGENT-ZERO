## Honesty note

No real-world competitor names, market-size figures, or supplier quotes were sourced for this — that was a deliberate choice to keep it self-contained rather than fabricate "researched" numbers. Every dollar figure, market-size estimate, and competitor comparison in the Investor and Government sections is explicitly marked as an **illustrative assumption to validate**, not a verified fact. Treat those numbers as a model to fill in, not a pitch to hand to a real investor or agency as-is.
