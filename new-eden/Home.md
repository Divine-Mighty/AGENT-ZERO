# New Eden — Vault Home

One Obsidian vault, back-engineered from the New Eden concept renders (the 30-feature residential line, the disaster-relief pod, and the robotic factory). Open this `new-eden/` folder as your Obsidian vault — every note below lives inside it and cross-links freely with `[[Wikilinks]]`.

Everything shares one locked ground-truth spec (dimensions, materials, manufacturing process, standards floor), so numbers are consistent across every section. Start with **Core** if you want the underlying facts; jump straight to whichever section matches what you're trying to decide.

## Honesty note

No real-world competitor names, market-size figures, or supplier quotes were sourced for this — that was a deliberate choice to keep it self-contained rather than fabricate "researched" numbers. Every dollar figure, market-size estimate, and competitor comparison in the Investor and Government sections is a **worked illustrative example**, internally consistent with the rest of the vault and explicitly flagged wherever it appears — not a verified fact. The one exception, on principle: [[Investor Home|01 · Investor Case]]'s "The Ask" leaves instrument, valuation, round stage, and close date as genuine open founder decisions rather than inventing negotiated terms — those aren't the kind of thing a cost model can responsibly fill in. Treat every other number as a model to replace with real data, not a pitch to hand to a real investor or agency as-is.
