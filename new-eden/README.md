# New Eden — Vault Index

Four independent Obsidian vaults, back-engineered from the New Eden concept renders (30-feature residential line, disaster-relief pod, robotic factory). Open any one of the four folders below directly as its own Obsidian vault — each is self-contained with its own `Home.md` entry point.

All four share the same locked ground-truth spec (dimensions, materials, manufacturing process, standards floor), so numbers are consistent across all of them. Start with the core vault if you want the underlying facts; jump straight to whichever audience-facing vault matches what you're trying to decide.

## The four vaults

| Vault | Folder | Open this if you want... |
|---|---|---|
| **Core Product & Manufacturing** | [`00-core-vault/`](./00-core-vault/Home.md) | The ground-truth reference: all 30 features back-engineered into real construction methods, the 8-stage robotic build process, factory/automation architecture, dimensions, materials, standards baseline, and the "exceed code, don't just meet it" design philosophy. |
| **Investor Case** | [`01-investor-vault/`](./01-investor-vault/Home.md) | The "no-brainer" investment thesis — moat, unit economics, scaling plan, risks, the ask. **All figures are illustrative placeholders, explicitly flagged — not real market research.** |
| **Founder Reality Check** | [`02-validation-vault/`](./02-validation-vault/Home.md) | The brutally honest feasibility audit — what has to be true, engineering risk, compliance risk, real capex categories, and the concrete next steps to actually validate this before spending money. |
| **Government / Disaster-Relief Case** | [`03-government-vault/`](./03-government-vault/Home.md) | The procurement-facing case for emergency-services buyers — deployment value, compliance for life-safety use, fleet/procurement model, total cost of ownership. |

## Reading order, if you want one

1. `00-core-vault` — get the facts straight first.
2. `02-validation-vault` — stress-test them before you believe the pitch.
3. `01-investor-vault` and `03-government-vault` — the two audience-facing "no-brainer" cases, once you know which claims in them are solid vs. illustrative.

## Honesty note

No real-world competitor names, market-size figures, or supplier quotes were sourced for this — that was a deliberate choice (per the founder) to keep this self-contained rather than fabricate "researched" numbers. Every dollar figure, market-size estimate, and competitor comparison in the investor and government vaults is explicitly marked as an **illustrative assumption to validate**, not a verified fact. Treat those numbers as a model to fill in, not a pitch to hand to a real investor or agency as-is.
