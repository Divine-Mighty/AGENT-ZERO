# New Eden — Investor Vault

New Eden is a premium modular-home brand built around a single 40ft Hi-Cube ISO-compatible module, manufactured on a robotic production line (ABB robotics + FrameCAD roll-forming) at "Factory 01," and sold through two channels off the same chassis: a design-led residential product ("30 New Ideas. One Vision.") and a government-procured disaster-relief rapid-response pod (NE-DR-401, "Deploy. Connect. Protect. Recover."). This vault lays out the investment case for that model — the structural argument for why it should work, an honest and explicitly unvalidated financial framework to stress-test before it's used in a real raise, and the risks that stand between the thesis and reality.

## Product reference (ground truth)

**Chassis** — 40ft Hi-Cube ISO module. External 12,192 x 2,438 x 2,896mm; internal 12,032 x 2,352 x 2,698mm. 8x ISO 1161 corner castings, stackable 3-high, road/rail/sea compatible, plug-and-play utility connections.

**Residential line** — "30 New Ideas. One Vision." — 30 named signature features spanning envelope details (floating roof, hidden gutters, vertical shadow gaps), structural/robotics design elements (robot lift points, exposed steel truss as a feature), energy systems (solar roof skin, hidden A/C, smart ceiling), premium finish options (copper in New/Weathered/Verdigris ageing states, woodgrain, expandable modules), and a factory digital-twin/QR traceability tag on every unit.

**Disaster Relief Pod (NE-DR-401)** — "Deploy. Connect. Protect. Recover." Tare weight ~6,800kg, deployment <30 min, role configurations Medical/Command/Accommodation/Support, 25+ year lifespan, rooftop solar, comms mast, branded for Australian emergency services (SES/ambulance context, "Dial 000").

**Materials** — Zinc Monument, Colorbond Woodland Grey (woodgrain), Copper Natural, Colorbond Night Sky.

**Manufacturing** — 8-stage "raw steel to finished home" line: ISO chassis → floor cassette → ABB-robot wall framing → roll-formed roof trusses → zinc/woodgrain cladding → standing-seam roof → interiors/MEP → QC & delivery. Marketed as "AI Powered Production | ABB Robotics | Digital Twin Monitoring | Zero Human Input."

**Factory** — purpose-built plant with real robotics/framing technology (ABB arms, FrameCAD roll-forming lines), branded "Factory 01" with per-unit digital-twin QR tracking built into the product from day one.

**Regulatory stance** — Australian NCC/AS-NZS standards are treated as the regulatory floor, not the target; the product is designed to exceed them as a deliberate premium play.
