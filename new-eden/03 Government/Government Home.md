# New Eden — Disaster Relief Pod: Government & Procurement Vault

This vault is a briefing set for emergency-management agencies, procurement officers, and civil contingency planners evaluating the **New Eden Disaster Relief Pod (NE-DR-401)** — the "Rapid Response Unit" variant of New Eden's modular building platform — for standing reserve fleets, regional depot staging, and field deployment.

> **Deploy. Connect. Protect. Recover.**

New Eden is a modular-home manufacturer building on a single 40ft Hi-Cube ISO-compatible chassis platform. The same 8-stage robotic factory process that produces New Eden's residential range also produces the NE-DR-401, engineered specifically for rapid emergency deployment: medical treatment space, incident command, displaced-persons accommodation, and logistics support — deployable in under 30 minutes, transportable by road, rail, or sea, and stackable three-high for compact depot storage.

This is not a marketing package. Every claim in this vault is scoped to what can be verified against Australian standards and stated engineering facts. Sections that model cost or compare against incumbent options are explicitly labelled as illustrative frameworks, not sourced benchmarking data — see the [[Comparison vs Incumbent Options]] and [[Total Cost of Ownership Model]] notes for those caveats.

## What the unit is

- **Platform:** 40ft Hi-Cube ISO-compatible module. External 12,192 × 2,438 × 2,896mm; internal 12,032 × 2,352 × 2,698mm.
- **Structure:** 8x ISO 1161 corner castings, engineered stackable 3-high, road/rail/sea freight compatible, plug-and-play utility connection system.
- **Tare weight (approx.):** 6,800kg
- **Deployment time:** under 30 minutes from arrival to operational
- **Lifespan:** 25+ years
- **Role configurations:** Medical / Command / Accommodation / Support
- **Fitted as standard:** rooftop solar panels, comms mast/antenna, entry awning, exterior power/data/plumbing service ports, first-aid signage, egress steps
- **Finishes:** Night Sky, Monument, Woodland Grey Woodgrain
- **Manufacturing:** same 8-stage robotic build process as the residential line (ABB robotic wall framing, roll-formed floor/trusses, zinc/woodgrain cladding, standing-seam roof, MEP and interior fit-out, QC and delivery), every unit factory-tagged with a QR digital-twin ID for lifecycle and inspection tracking

## Founder position on standards

New Eden's stated position: Australian NCC and AS/NZS standards are treated as the **regulatory floor, not the target**. For a residential product that is already a differentiator; for a life-safety emergency-response product deployed into acute conditions — cyclones, floods, bushfire evacuation zones — by definition the moment it is needed most is the moment conditions are worst. That raises, rather than lowers, the bar the unit needs to clear above minimum code compliance. This position is expanded in [[Compliance for Emergency Life-Safety Use]].
