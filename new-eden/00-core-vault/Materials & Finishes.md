# Materials & Finishes

New Eden uses a fixed four-material palette across both [[Product Line - Residential]] and [[Product Line - Disaster Relief Pod]], reinforcing the "Signature architecture" brand discipline described in [[Product Line - Residential]] feature 30. Within that fixed palette, copper carries a customer-selectable ageing-finish option that functions as the brand's primary premium customization and margin lever.

## The four-material palette

**Zinc Monument** — a zinc/zinc-alloy-finish steel cladding (the naming convention matches real Australian architectural steel product lines, e.g. COLORBOND® "Monument" is a very dark near-black; "Zinc Monument" reads as a zinc-finish equivalent in that dark tonal family). Used as the dominant body-cladding material across most elevations, forming the neutral backdrop that the woodgrain and copper accent features (see [[Product Line - Residential]] features 6, 7) are set against. Zinc's real-world advantage relevant here: a durable, low-maintenance, naturally corrosion-resistant finish suited to the magnetic cladding panel system (feature 22) since it can be roll-formed and factory-finished consistently at panel scale.

**Colorbond Woodland Grey (woodgrain finish)** — a woodgrain-effect finish applied to Colorbond-class pre-finished steel (Colorbond is a real Australian pre-painted steel product line; "Woodland Grey" as named here functions as a specific woodgrain-effect colourway within that family). Used for the woodgrain feature box (feature 6) and other accent volumes, giving a natural-material visual read while retaining steel's durability, bushfire performance, and factory-finish consistency — critical because a real timber cladding would compromise both the bushfire-prone-area performance target (see [[Standards & Compliance Baseline]]) and the robotic panel-placement tolerance the line is built around (see [[Factory & Automation Stack]]).

**Copper Natural** — architectural copper sheet, roll-formed/folded for use on the copper portal (feature 7) and related accent trims, specified in one of three ageing-finish options (below). Requires galvanic isolation detailing from the steel/zinc chassis, as noted in [[Product Line - Residential]] feature 7.

**Colorbond Night Sky** — a deep black/near-black pre-finished steel colourway, most plausibly used for the black steel base/plinth zone (feature 8) and other dark accent elements, distinct from Zinc Monument by being a painted/pre-finished coil product rather than a bare/near-bare zinc finish — giving New Eden two distinct "dark" materials in its palette (a painted black and a metallic dark zinc) that read differently under different light, which is a deliberate architectural materiality choice rather than redundancy.

## Copper ageing options — the premium customization lever

Real architectural copper visibly changes appearance over years to decades as it oxidizes: bright "new copper" pinkish-orange gradually dulls and browns, then develops a green-blue patina (verdigris) over roughly 15–20+ years outdoors under normal weathering, depending on climate and exposure. New Eden offers this natural process as three factory-selectable finish options rather than one fixed material:

- **New Copper** — bright, freshly-finished copper appearance, either genuinely raw/lacquered copper (which will itself begin ageing on site over subsequent years unless clear-coated) or a factory finish calibrated to hold that bright look longer than bare copper naturally would.
- **Weathered** — a mid-life brown/dulled copper appearance, most plausibly achieved via a factory chemical patination process (accelerated oxidation treatments are a standard architectural-metal technique) rather than waiting years for natural weathering, letting a customer buy a "20-year-aged" look on a brand-new build.
- **Verdigris** — the mature blue-green patina appearance, again most plausibly factory-applied via accelerated chemical patination (verdigris-effect patination finishes are a real, commercially available architectural metal treatment) so a customer can select the fully-aged look immediately rather than only reaching it after decades in service.

### Why this is a genuine margin lever, not just a colour option
Because all three options are almost certainly achievable through different factory finishing processes applied to the same base copper sheet (rather than three different raw materials), the input material cost is close to identical across the three choices — the price differential a premium brand can charge is driven by the specialized patination process and the design cachet of "instant maturity" (an architectural look that would otherwise require decades to develop naturally), which is a high-margin, low-marginal-cost customization axis exactly suited to a configure-to-order manufacturing model like New Eden's (see [[Factory & Automation Stack]]).

### Maintenance implication worth flagging to other vaults
A factory-patinated "Weathered" or "Verdigris" finish is a static, sealed surface treatment — it will not continue to age the same way unprotected natural copper does, and any future repair/patch work (e.g. after magnetic cladding panel replacement — see [[Product Line - Residential]] feature 22, or [[Product Line - Disaster Relief Pod]] field service) needs colour-matched patination touch-up capability, not just plain copper stock, to avoid a visibly mismatched repair. This should be considered a service-network capability requirement in the investor/operations vaults, not just a factory finishing detail.
