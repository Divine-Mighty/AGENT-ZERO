# New Eden — Validation Vault (The Reality Check)

This vault exists for one person: the founder, before any real money moves. Every other New Eden vault — [[Brand & Positioning|core]], investor, government — is written to make the idea look as strong as its facts allow. This one is written to break it. If a claim in this vault survives, it's because it actually held up, not because it was left untested.

The ground truth (dimensions, the 8-stage line, the 30 residential features, the NE-DR-401 pod, the four-material palette) is treated as fixed and is not re-litigated here — see the core vault for that. What *is* litigated here: does any of it actually work when it meets a welding tolerance, a bushfire assessment, a bank account, or an SES procurement officer?

## Start here
- [[Reality Check Summary]] — the single most important note in this vault. A short, falsifiable go/no-go checklist: what has to be true for New Eden to survive contact with reality. Read this first and read it again before spending a dollar.

## The four pressure tests
- [[Engineering Feasibility]] — walking the 8 manufacturing stages honestly: where robotic precision is genuinely achievable, where "robot built" is doing more marketing work than engineering work, and what "Zero Human Input" actually means once you get past framing.
- [[Compliance Feasibility]] — where the AS/NZS standard families that govern this product create real tension with the design language (bushfire construction vs. hidden gutters and glazing; wind loading vs. a lightweight relocatable steel box; accessibility vs. a fixed 2,352mm internal width; wiring/gas rules vs. exterior plug-and-play service ports).
- [[Cost-to-Build Reality Check]] — the capex categories nobody puts on a pitch deck: the robotics cell, the factory shell, the digital-twin software stack, certification. Blunt read on why this is a capital-intensive bet and what has to be true about volume for that bet to pay off.
- [[Open Questions & Next Validation Steps]] — the concrete, unglamorous punch list of what to go verify next, in order, before spending real capital.

## How to use this vault
- This vault does not introduce new ground-truth facts and does not contradict the core vault's dimensions, feature list, or process description. It interrogates them.
- Where this vault says something is unproven, it means genuinely unproven — no real-world supplier quotes, competitor benchmarking, or market research has been done for this concept. Cost and timeline judgments here are directional and reasoning-based, not sourced figures. Anywhere a specific number appears, it is there to make an assumption falsifiable, not because it has been verified.
- Standards are referenced by family (AS 3959, AS/NZS 1170.2, AS/NZS 4600, AS 1428, AS/NZS 3000, AS/NZS 5601) at the level of "this governs X and here is the tension," not by clause number — nobody has had this design in front of a certifying engineer yet.
- If a claim in the brand or investor vaults reads as settled and a claim in here reads as shaky, believe this vault. That asymmetry is the point of it existing.
